How to Play

  • 1

    Select Chord

    Hold up your left index finger over a segment on the radial wheel to lock in your active chord (C, G, F, Am, Em, Dm).

  • 2

    Pluck & Strum Strings

    Sweep your right index finger across the 6 vertical string lines on the right to strum, or pluck individual strings for melody!

  • 3

    Controls

    Press D on your keyboard to toggle hand tracking debug skeletons.
