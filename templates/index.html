How to Play

  • 1

    Select Chord

    Hold up your left hand index finger. Move the pointer over a segment on the chord wheel and hover for 300ms to lock it in.

  • 2

    Pluck & Strum Strings

    Move your right hand index finger over the 6 vertical strings on the right. Sweep across them to strum, or cross individual strings to pluck specific notes!

  • 3

    Hotkeys

    Press D in the terminal to toggle tracking skeletons overlay, or C to cycle camera sources.
